# Wrist Metronome – Support

Wrist Metronome is a metronome you feel as well as hear. It gives you gentle haptic taps on Apple Watch, or a clear click on Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6741377595)

## Features

### Apple Watch
- **Feel the beat.** Crisp haptic taps let you keep time without looking.
- **Keeps going with your wrist down.** The metronome keeps running while the screen sleeps.
- **Digital Crown control.** Set any tempo from 30 to 240 BPM, even while it's playing.
- **Quick presets.** Tap the dial to jump between common tempos.
- **Haptic or sound.** Switch between silent haptics and an audible click.
- **Remembers your settings.** Your last tempo and mode are kept.

### iPhone and iPad
- **Tempo dial.** Drag the dial, or use the presets and the + / − buttons.
- **Live tempo changes.** Adjust the tempo while it plays.
- **Background playback.** It keeps playing with the screen locked.
- **Practice profiles.** Save, rename, load and delete your tempos.
- **Light and dark mode.** It follows the system setting, or you can choose one in the toolbar.
- **iPad.** A profiles sidebar, Split View and every orientation are supported.

## How to use

### Apple Watch
1. **Set the tempo.** Turn the Digital Crown, or tap the dial to step through the presets.
2. **Start and stop.** Tap the centre button: a **green ▶ means Play** and a **red ■ means Stop**. A ring pulses on every beat.
3. **Choose the output.** Tap the button below the dial to switch between **Haptic** and **Sound**.
4. **Lower your wrist.** The metronome keeps going. A session runs for **up to 1 hour** at a time; when it ends, the app tells you, and you can press Play to start again.

### iPhone and iPad
1. **Set the tempo.** Drag the dial, tap a preset or use + / −.
2. **Start and stop.** Tap **Play** (green) or **Stop** (red).
3. **Save a profile.** Tap the save button in the toolbar and give it a name.
4. **Manage profiles.** Open **Profiles**: it's the list button on iPhone and the sidebar on iPad. Tap a profile to load it. Swipe it, or touch and hold, to rename or delete it.

## Troubleshooting

**The watch metronome stops when I lower my wrist**
- Turn on **Background App Refresh** for Wrist Metronome. You can do this on the watch in Settings › General › Background App Refresh, or in the Watch app on your iPhone. The app shows a message if it is off.
- Sessions last up to 1 hour. Press Play again to continue.

**No haptic taps**
- Check that the output is set to **Haptic**.
- Check the watch's haptic strength in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
- Above 200 BPM, haptic mode taps on every second beat, because the watch can't tap reliably any faster.

**No sound**
- Check that the output is set to **Sound** on the watch.
- Turn up the volume. Wrist Metronome plays its click even when Silent Mode is on.

**The metronome stopped with a message**
- A phone call, Siri, or a change of headphones or speaker can interrupt the sound. Press Play to start again.

**The tempo won't change on the watch**
- Make sure the app is open and in focus, then turn the Digital Crown.
