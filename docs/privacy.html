Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 (replaces the version dated 2 February 2025)

In short: Wrist Metronome does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your last tempo, time signature and output mode (haptic or sound),
  • your appearance and display settings,
  • the practice profiles you create on iPhone or iPad (a name, a tempo and the date saved).

This data never leaves your devices. The only exceptions are your own device backups (such as iCloud Backup), which Apple manages, and syncing between your own paired Apple Watch and iPhone: the tempo, time signature, play state and profile names and tempos are sent directly between them so the two apps stay in step. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Health data

Wrist Metronome does not access or use Apple Health (HealthKit) data, and it does not start workouts.

Third-party services

The app does not use or share data with any third-party services or SDKs.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party resources. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
