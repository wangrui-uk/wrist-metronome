# Wrist Metronome

**Feel the beat on your wrist.**

Wrist Metronome is a metronome you feel as well as hear. You can keep time with gentle haptic taps on Apple Watch, or with a clear click on Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. It's simple enough for daily practice and steady enough for rehearsal and performance.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6741377595)

## Why Wrist Metronome?

- **Feel it, don't just hear it.** Haptic taps keep you in time silently, with no earbuds and no looking at a screen.
- **Rock-steady timing.** Every beat is scheduled precisely, so the tempo doesn't drift at 40 BPM or at 240.
- **Keeps going with your wrist down.** Lower your arm and play. The metronome keeps running while the watch screen sleeps, and on iPhone and iPad it keeps playing with the screen locked.
- **Change tempo on the fly.** Turn the Digital Crown or drag the dial while it plays. There's no need to stop and restart.
- **Your practice tempos, saved.** Create profiles on iPhone and iPad for each piece, exercise or song.
- **Designed for every screen.** It works on Apple Watch, on iPhone in portrait and landscape, and on iPad with a sidebar and Split View, in light or dark mode.
- **Accessible.** It supports VoiceOver and large text throughout.
- **Private.** There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and no data is collected.

## Who is it for?

- 🎹 **Pianists and classical players**: Largo to Prestissimo, with tempo markings shown as you change speed.
- 🎸 **Guitarists and bassists**: lock in your strumming and picking.
- 🥁 **Drummers**: feel the click on your wrist without it bleeding into the mics.
- 💃 **Dancers and fitness**: count beats and reps without a sound.

## How it works

1. Set your tempo with the Digital Crown, the dial or a preset.
2. Press **Play**.
3. Feel the beat on your wrist, or hear the click.
